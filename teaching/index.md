# Teaching Experience

## DIG 2030 Digital Video Fundamentals

- Introduces digital video production from preproduction through final publication, including storyboarding, filming, lighting, sound recording, and editing with Adobe Premiere Pro.
- Fall 2022

## DIG 4630 Creative Industries

- Examines the business of creative media industries, including market structures, funding models, business planning, competitive analysis, production management, and pitching digital content.
- Fall 2022, Spring 2023

## DIG 3728C Game Testing

- Introduces professional game-testing and quality-assurance practices, including test planning, bug tracking, certification processes, interface standards, and technical testing.
- Spring 2023

## DIG 3525 Digital Media Production I

- Covers the planning and management of team-based digital media projects, including production pipelines, scheduling, budgeting, communication, and the development of a project management plan.
- Fall 2025

## DIG 3716C Client-Side Scripting

- Introduces client-side web development using JavaScript, covering interactive websites, user experience, information architecture, data handling, and web application development.
- Fall 2025, Fall 2026

## DIG 3134C Server-Side Scripting

- Introduces server-side web development using PHP, MySQL, and XML, including database design, CRUD operations, and dynamic content management systems.
- Fall 2025, Spring 2026, Fall 2026

## DIG 3480 Computer as a Medium

- Introduces coding and scripting in modern game engines, covering 2D and 3D game development, game functionality, and the integration of art and sound assets.
- Fall 2025, Fall 2026

## DIG 4639C Mobile Development

- Introduces mobile application development using JavaScript, React, and React Native, with an emphasis on mobile-first design, debugging, user interfaces, and portfolio development.
- Spring 2026

## DIG 4941 Undergraduate Internship

- Provides students with supervised professional experience where they can apply their skills, develop workplace communication and explore career opportunities in their internship.
- Fall 2026
